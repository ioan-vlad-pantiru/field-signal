What we build on top

Two derived tables carry the labels; everything above lands as raw long-format Parquet and is joined at analysis time.

points.parquet — one row per point

point_id, lon, lat, country, nuts3, county, holdout, cell_id, modal_crop, inclusion_probability

Sampled as 300,000 points stratified by country × modal crop, with a floor per stratum, one point per 250 m cell to avoid pseudo-replication, and each point's inclusion probability stored. Points in Alba, Cluj, Mureș, Sibiu and Hunedoara are flagged holdout=True.

crops.parquet — one row per point-year

point_id, year, crop, confidence, purity_m250, flags

Every year 2017–2024 is stored for every point, including non-crop and low-confidence years. Fallow and temporary grass are rotation states, not missing data. purity_m250 is the share of the containing 250 m MODIS cell growing that point's crop.

Raw files are long format: point_id, date_or_year, variable, value. The only exception is power and smap_sm, keyed by cell_id — expanding a daily 1981 series to 300k points would be roughly 1010 identical rows.

Gotchas that make identical sources look different

Masked pixels disappear as rows

Earth Engine omits a masked pixel entirely rather than returning NaN, so "0 % missing" overstates coverage. Compare row counts against points × dates — that is how the 5,625 missing HLS months were found.

HLS S30 has B8A, not B8

S30 is harmonised to Sentinel-2 apart from the red and red-edge bands. An NDVI computed with B8 is silently wrong; ours uses B8A paired with L30 B5.

SMAP is version 008

SPL4SMGP/008, not 007. The two are different products and are not interchangeable in a comparison.

HRL confidence sentinels

253 and 255 sit above any plausible threshold. Treating them as percentages makes every "no cropland" pixel look maximally confident.

Two POWER grids

Most variables are on 0.5° × 0.625°; ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN is on 1° and starts in 1984, so 1981–1983 solar is genuinely absent rather than zero.

Embedding is annual and tiled

One mosaic per year; years present are probed rather than hardcoded, which is how 2025 was found to exist even though the catalogue text stops at 2024.

What we deliberately do not do

  • No imputation, interpolation or gap filling — a missing value stays missing.
  • No regridding to a common raster; each dataset keeps its native grid and scale.
  • No confidence, purity or quality filtering at build time. All of it is stored so filters remain reversible choices.
  • No cross-dataset joins baked into the files. cell_id and point_id are the join keys.
  • No synthetic or derived labels beyond the HRL class mapping and geometric derivations (slope, purity, composites).
